<YO:song>
Convergence of the essence of Gyeonggi folk songs and modern reinterpretation

Lee Hee-moon is a unique artist in reinterpreting Korean traditional music in a modern way, opening a new path for traditional folk songs. "YoYO: song" will be a musical experiment and a place of communication that transcends tradition and modernity, Eastern and Western styles, unraveling the sentiment of traditional music in modern language.
<Yo:song> is a work that creates the beauty of Gyeonggi folk songs with a modern sense.
I hope you experience the deep reverberation and emotion contained in each song.

Vocal: Lee Hee-moon  
Janggu: Park Beom-tae  
Modular Synthesizer: Lim Yong-ju  
Piri: Choi Kwang Il
Haegeum: Won Na-Kyeong
