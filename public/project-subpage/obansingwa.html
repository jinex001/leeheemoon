OBSG

"Confusion, utter confusion! The Five Directional Gods will rescue you from the suffering and anguish of this world!"

*ObangShin-gwa (OBSG)* is a performance in which *Gyeonggi Sori* artist Lee Hee-moon transforms into the *ObangShin* (Five Directional Gods) and leads the audience on an escape from the mundane world. Breaking the boundaries between tradition and modernity, this dynamic show features *NomNom*, the "idols of Joseon," and *HeoSongSaeWol*, a band led by No Sun-taek, coming together to present a unique and unconventional take on *minyo* (folk songs).
