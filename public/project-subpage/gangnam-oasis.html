Lee Hee Moon Songform stories 'GangNam'

The Gangnam Series by Sorikkun Lee Hee-moon is a trilogy project that explores his life before he fully immersed himself in traditional Korean folk singing (minyo). This series is based on his experiences and stories from his twenties, spent in the Gangnam area. The project blends tradition and modernity, offering an artistic portrayal of the life he lived before discovering and engaging with minyo. It provides insight into his journey, capturing the essence of his youthful years in Gangnam before his transition into the world of Korean folk music.
This series offers an opportunity to understand Lee Hee-moon's unique musical style and to delve deeper into his life and inner world before he embarked on his journey with traditional Korean folk singing.
