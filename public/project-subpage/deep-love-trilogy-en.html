Deep Love trilogy

The series performance *Deep Sarang*, presented over three years starting in 2016, explores the history and culture of *Gyeonggi Minyo* (Gyeonggi folk songs).  
‍
Part 1: Deep Love – A performance showcasing the essence of *Gyeonggi Minyo*.
 
Part 2: The Axis of Four Seasons – A narrative capturing the seasonal changes and rhythms of life in *Gyeonggi Minyo*.  
‍
Part 3: Folk Songs Across the Land – A storytelling-based performance intertwining *Gyeonggi Minyo* and *Japga* (traditional narrative songs).  

This performance series delves into the diverse aspects of *Gyeonggi Minyo*, demonstrating that tradition is not merely something to be preserved but can be reinterpreted with a modern sensibility.
