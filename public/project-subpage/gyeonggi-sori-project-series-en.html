Gyeonggi Sori project series

The *Gyeonggi Sori Project* aims to establish traditional vocal music as a sustainable repertoire rather than being consumed as a one-time performance. Lee Hee-moon preserves the unique characteristics of *Gyeonggi Sori* while incorporating contemporary sensibilities by blending various musical elements. Through distinctive stage production and dramatic devices, he presents a new direction for performances, enabling traditional music to actively engage with modern audiences.
