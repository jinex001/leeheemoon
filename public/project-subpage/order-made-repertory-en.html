Order made repertory

Breaking Tradition, Tailoring a New Fit.

If traditional performances can be compared to "ready-to-wear" clothing, an order-made repertoire is like "custom-tailored" attire infused with contemporary sensibilities. *Gyeonggi Sori* artist Lee Hee-moon breaks away from the fixed formats of traditional performances, presenting a new form of performance that actively reflects the currents of the modern era, as well as the individuality of the creator and the evolving spirit of the times.
