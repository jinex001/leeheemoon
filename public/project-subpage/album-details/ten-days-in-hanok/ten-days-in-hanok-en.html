TEN DAYS in HANOK
Ten Days in Hanok

Over ten days, a complete performance of Gyeonggi 12 Japga takes place in a traditional hanok, conveying the prototype and artistic depth of Gyeonggi vocal music while sharing the beauty of traditional song harmonized with the spatial presence of the hanok.Through the story shared by folk singer Lee Hee-mun, audiences can gain a deeper understanding of the emotions embedded in Gyeonggi 12 Japga and his personal narrative built through music. Beyond a simple listening experience, the audience engages with the background and aesthetics of the work, encountering the meaning of Gyeonggi vocal music in a multi-dimensional way.This performance reflects on the traditional depth of Gyeonggi 12 Japga and invites audiences to experience its contemporary significance as a living, modern art form.
Inspiration Talk
Bringing the Old into the Today

Gyeonggi Minyo (folk song) vocalist Lee Hee-moon engages in conversations with an architect and a fashion art director, reflecting on the intersections of voice and space, and voice and attire.Though vocal music, spatial design, and fashion belong to distinct disciplines, they all express how human beings experience life and capture the spirit of their era. Bringing old traditions and familiar motifs into contemporary context, these three creators share their creative processes, philosophy, and personal journeys.What to preserve, and what to transform; how the familiar evolves into fresh artistic sensations.
Isaac Jeong, Architect
Younghee Seo, Fashion Art Director
