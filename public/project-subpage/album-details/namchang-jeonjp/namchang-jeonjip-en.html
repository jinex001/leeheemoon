NAMCHANG-JEONJIP
Namchang-jeonjip
Facing the Present of Gyeonggi Sori

The most unconventional sorikkun (singer) Lee Heemoon × Poet of space and stage designer Yeo Shin-dong.A sensory encounter that shatters the boundaries of tradition!
‍
Crossing genres and leaping over generations,The depth of a veteran master meets the energy of rising vocalists.
‍
To sing the "Gyeonggi Sori" that breathes in this very moment.

The Aesthetics of a Moment Where Senses IntersectUpon a modern space envisioned by Yeo Shin-dong, The powerful Namchang (male vocals) of Lee Heemoon and emerging singers declare:“We shall not leave this life without play.”

At the instant where different times and sensibilities collide,The Pyeong-sijo (standard verse) of Naraet-garak and the Janghyeong-sijo (extended verse) of Changbu-taryeongFinally become the “Songs of Today.”

Vocalists: Lee Heemoon, Lee Chae-hyun, Jeong Jun-pilStage Design: Yeo Shin-dongInstrumentalists: Lee Min-hyung (Janggu), Lee Chan-woo (Piri), Byeon Sang-yeop (Daegeum), Kim Seung-tae (Haegeum), Bae Ho-young (Ajaeng), Chu Hyeon-tak (Gayageum)
