Minyo!
The spin-off Minyo! of the 1968 album Minyo Samcheonri tells the story of "Minyo," a character created by the collaboration between traditional Gyeonggi sori vocalist Lee Hee-moon, music arranger Itaewon, and visual director Woo Sang-hee. As the final piece of the three-part series Deep Love, Minyo! adapts and reimagines the 1968 album Minyo Samcheonri, originally produced by the National Gugak Center. For traditional vocalists of the time, Minyo Samcheonriwas akin to a textbook, capturing a comprehensive collection of folk songs from across Korea. Its reputation reflected the popularity of minyo in the 1960s, a time when these folk songs were still close to the hearts of the public. Today, many of those beloved folk songs have faded from memory. This spin-off, Minyo!, is inspired by the theme of "Minyo has disappeared" and portrays Lee Hee-moon's dream of seeking out the lost minyo in a new online concert.
