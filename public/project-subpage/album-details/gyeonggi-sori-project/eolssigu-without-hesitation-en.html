Eolssigu, without Hesitation
Another Gyeonggi Sori project by Lee and premiering in 2012, this performance emerged from curiosity as to what would happen if a stage were set up for stay-at-home mothers. Traditional Gyeonggi minyo songs are reinterpreted in a contemporary sense and enjoyable for all.
